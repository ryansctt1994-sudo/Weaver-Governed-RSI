# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation requires respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration. Critique claims,
methods, code, and threat models directly; do not attack people or conceal uncertainty.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- distinguishing observations from interpretations;
- publishing reproducible failures without triumphalism;
- disclosing conflicts of interest and identity overlap;
- protecting reporters and sensitive vulnerability details;
- correcting the record when evidence changes.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential exposure, fabricated evidence, and pressure
to overstate findings are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove content or
participation privileges when necessary to protect contributors or the integrity of the
research record.
